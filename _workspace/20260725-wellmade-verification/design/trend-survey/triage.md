## Bounded question

> For each of Abyssal Command's five candidate novelty elements, in how many of ≥5
> comparable defense-survivor / auto-battler / roguelite-survivor titles does an
> equivalent element appear?

G8 threshold: an element qualifies if it appears in **≤2 of ≥5** surveyed titles AND
carries a **QA impression score ≥4/5**. Frequency is this survey's job; the impression
half is QA's and is tracked separately.

## Comparable-set inclusion rule (fixed before searching)

A title is comparable if it satisfies both:
1. auto-resolved or near-auto-resolved combat against continuous enemy waves, and
2. a run/session structure with in-run power growth.

Titles were drawn from the two sub-families the build straddles — PC/Steam
roguelite-survivor and mobile defense/4X-hybrid — because Abyssal Command is a
survivor-shaped run loop wrapped in a gate-defense objective chain. 11 titles surveyed,
above the ≥5 floor.

## Adversarial stance (self-imposed)

The assignment requires it and it is the right posture: **if every candidate appears in
>2 of 5 surveyed titles, the honest verdict is G8 FAIL.** No candidate is promoted on
narrative appeal. Where a candidate passes on frequency but the evidence is an absence
of found examples rather than a confirmed negative, that is labelled as such and the
candidate is downgraded, not inflated.

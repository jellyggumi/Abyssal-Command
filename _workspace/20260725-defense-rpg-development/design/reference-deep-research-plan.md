# Reference Deep Research Plan

## Question
What officially disclosed patterns from six relevant comparators can improve a readable, deterministic 3D defense/offense + RPG vertical slice without copying their rules, presentation, or claiming player evidence?

## Source discipline
- Prefer official publisher/developer pages or official support pages.
- Record one public-description observation per title, then label each local proposal as a **transferable inference**.
- Do not treat public copy as a runtime trace, usability result, or human-impression evidence.

## Parallel subtopics
1. **Telegraphing and tactical legibility:** Into the Breach and XCOM; expected output: disclosure-backed pattern for visible threats, routes, and distinct tactical choices.
2. **Run pacing and responsive action:** Hades and Dead Cells; expected output: disclosure-backed pattern for a short action loop and explicit run-to-progress handoff.
3. **Expedition risk and targeted-hunt reward:** Darkest Dungeon and Monster Hunter; expected output: disclosure-backed pattern for visible failure consequence and a named reward handoff.

## Synthesis
The final `reference-deep-research.md` will map each bounded observation to Cinder Span, name an explicit non-copy constraint, and link every proposal to a future test rather than treating it as shipped evidence.

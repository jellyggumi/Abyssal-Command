# Lobby Concept — UI Senior Developer Position

Lane: UI-senior-developer · Scope: the 출정 (sortie/launch) surface only · Grounded in `adoptable-pattern-shortlist.md` (A1–A6 + hard constraints), `benchmark-notes.md` (calibration takeaways), `vampire-survivors-stage-selection.jpg` / `-collection.jpg` / `-itch-browser-menu.png`, and the live code (`renderDockRight`/`renderSortieTabBody`/`renderSortieFab` app.js L784–923, `styles.css` dock rules L21–142).

## Position

The lobby's real UI problem is not "not enough theater" — it is that the **theater budget is wildly asymmetric across the two viewports we actually ship, and the current layout treats them the same**. Measured from `styles.css` L54, the 출정 panel is `clamp(15rem, 70vw, 21rem)`: on a 390×844 phone (compact tier, one panel at a time) it renders at **273px and leaves only a ~61px sliver of live canvas** beside the 56px opposite rail, while on a 2056×1082 desktop both docks cap at 336px and frame a **~1384px live-canvas hero**. So the direction I back is: **make the live canvas the framed loadout-theater stage on desktop/wide (full A6 emit/parallax), and on mobile make theater panel-internal + gate the "see the world" moment to the dock-closed state** — because with the sortie panel open on a phone the scene is barely a strip, and pretending otherwise produces exactly the "pasted-on panel" failure A6 warns against. The two structural gaps I'd fix first are legibility-shaped, not decoration-shaped: (1) there is **no persistent currency readout in the lobby chrome at all** today (A5 is simply missing, not under-styled), and (2) the 출정 briefing is a flat 5-row `<dl>` (app.js L800–806) that buries "다음 보상" at row 5 — the one thing `context.md` says players actually read — under terrain lore they skip. Everything else (idle-return theater A1, stage-list-as-grid↔detail A4) layers cleanly onto the dock we already have without relocating a single learned control.

## Concrete lobby changes

- **Add a persistent screen-space currency rail (implements A5, currently 100% absent).** Two framed pills only — `Echo Core` (EC) and `Bound Fragment` (BF), the only two real persistent currencies in the model (`echoCoreEarned/Spent`, `boundFragmentEarned/Spent`, app.js L468–472). Anchor **top-left in pre-run screen space** (not top-center — that zone belongs to the idle toast, `styles.css` L129), each pill ≥48dp tall so tapping it deep-links into where it's spent (EC→성장 dock, BF→인벤토리 dock). It must be pre-run-only and yield the top corners to the battle HUD once `data-defense-started="true"`. This is the "reward readout the player passes constantly" from benchmark Takeaway 2 and every image-backed benchmark's top-corner gold pill (`vampire-survivors-stage-selection.jpg`).
- **Cap the currency rail at 2 pills — do NOT match Soulstone's 6-currency header.** `soulstone-survivors-skilltree.jpg` shows a dense multi-currency strip; importing that count is literally the "currency soup" that is the #1 documented complaint (constraint #4). Two pills, no more.
- **Reshape the 출정 briefing to lead with the payoff (progressive disclosure, constraint #4 + "briefings skipped" finding).** Keep boss portrait + `bossName` + `selectedObjective` + **다음 보상 promoted to the top line**; fold the four terrain/hazard/flank/landmark rows (`<dl class="briefing-stats">` app.js L800–806) behind a `전술 상세` disclosure. `context.md` L39: briefings are read "only if ≤ a few seconds and carrying actionable info (what/where the reward is)" — so answer "what does this buy me right now" inline, defer the lore.
- **Make the stage rail a grid↔detail read, not 5 equal rows (A4, `vampire-survivors-collection.jpg` + `-stage-selection.jpg` center list).** The selected `.stage-card` becomes the expanded visual anchor (art + boss + state); unselected stages compact to icon + index + name + locked/cleared chip. This is the exact icon-grid ↔ selected-detail idiom from A4/Takeaway 5, applied to the existing `stage-rail` (app.js L788–795) without adding a screen.
- **Keep START as the persistent bottom-center FAB — do not move it into the panel (A1-adjacent, constraint #6).** `renderSortieFab` (app.js L900) at `bottom: max(.75rem, safe-area)`, `min-height:56px`, `width: min(92vw, 26rem)` is already the thumb-zone, ≥48dp, one-unmistakable-CTA bar the benchmark demands (`vampire-survivors-itch-browser-menu.png`: single prominent START). Polish target only: sub-100ms scale + one pooled-particle tap burst on press, reduced-motion parity.
- **Theatricalize idle-return in place, top-anchored, with a handoff into the currency pills (A1, the highest-impact already-owned system).** Upgrade the existing top-center toast (`renderIdleReturnToast` app.js L930, `max-height:20vh`) into an eased count-up recap of accrued EC/BF, then animate the pile "landing" into the new top-left currency pills. Stays auto-dismiss (never a modal gate), stays top so the bottom FAB is always a clear one-thumb reach. This is the loved "payday" from `solutions.md` L38 — a reward for returning, NOT a login leash.
- **Realize A6 (docks emit from the canvas) as a viewport-tiered, cheap-motion behavior.** Desktop/wide: panels slide out of the canvas edge with subtle parallax against the live scene. Mobile: since the open panel hides ~85% of the canvas, make the **slide-out/in motion itself the emit read** and treat the dock-*closed* (rail-only, ~334px canvas) state as the canonical "see the world" state. Tweens + pooled sprite-FX only, within the single Three.js thread (constraint #2).

## What the mockup should show

Render the **desktop/wide state (≈16:9, this is where the loadout-theater reads fully)** with a labeled mobile inset:

- **Center (~two-thirds width): the live 3D canvas as the framed hero** — the frozen Cinder Span scene with the Cinder Warden silhouette previewed in it (the canvas IS the battlefield preview per hud-layout-spec, app.js L780–783), lit in canon palette: void-obsidian #3c2c5b base, cold-steel #737990 midtones, a cyan-rift #2cadd6 edge glow where the panels meet the scene, cinder-ember #f3592c embers drifting from the boss. Not a flat backdrop — the panels visibly emit from its left/right edges (A6).
- **Right wing (~336px): the 출정 panel**, glass gradient `linear-gradient(155deg, rgb(38 26 58/.82), rgb(16 10 28/.88))`. Top: `AC` brand + [출정 | 요새] tab switch + close. Below: a vertical **stage rail where the SELECTED stage (재의 봉쇄선) is an expanded card** (thumbnail art + boss name + "선택됨") and the others are compact rows with 잠김/CLEAR chips. Under it a tight briefing block **leading with a zenith-gold #ddc869 "다음 보상" line**, boss portrait + objective, and a collapsed `전술 상세 ▸` disclosure (terrain/hazard folded away).
- **Left wing (~336px):** the 성장 rail/panel edge, shown only to prove both docks frame the canvas symmetrically (out of scope, keep it dim).
- **Top-left screen space: two framed currency pills** — a cyan-rimmed `◈ Echo Core` pill and a gold-rimmed `✦ Bound Fragment` pill, each with a number, floating over the canvas corner (not inside a panel).
- **Bottom-center: the persistent `작전 개시` FAB** — wide cyan-bordered bar (`#81d6ff`), "작전 개시" primary + "재의 봉쇄선 · Cinder Warden 전선으로" sublabel + "↗", sitting in the thumb zone clear of everything above it.
- **Top-center: an idle-return recap card mid-count-up** (e.g. "귀환 · Echo Core +N") with an eased-reveal glow and a faint trail arcing toward the top-left EC pill, showing the A1→A5 handoff. It occupies only the top ~170px and never touches the FAB.
- **Mobile inset (390×844, labeled "compact — sortie open"):** show the 273px 출정 panel filling most of the width, the 56px left rail, and only a **thin ~61px canvas glow-strip** between them — with a callout that mobile theater is panel-internal (card motion + tap feedback) and the full scene appears only when the dock is closed. This asymmetry is the point of the mockup.

## Risks / disagreements

- **vs Designer (most likely clash): "living scene everywhere."** I expect the designer to want the spatial/diegetic canvas theater on every viewport. I disagree for mobile: with the sortie panel open at 273px there is a ~61px canvas sliver — a spatial scene read is physically impossible there, so theater must be panel-internal and the "place" moment gated to the dock-closed state. I also expect pushback on folding the briefing rows — but the 5-row `<dl>` always-visible is exactly the info-wall `context.md` flags. Resolution: lead-with-reward + progressive disclosure, not maximal exposure.
- **vs PM: currency count and retention hooks.** If PM wants more always-on readouts (extra pills, a daily-login streak) for engagement, I'll hold the line at **2 currency pills** (anti-"currency soup", constraint #4) and **idle-return-as-reward, not a login leash** (documented backlash, `solutions.md` L37, shortlist "adopt-with-caution"). A daily hook, if ever added, ties to "first sortie of the day," never a bare login.
- **vs Programmer: thread budget.** The count-up recap, canvas parallax, and tap-particle bursts all share one Three.js thread with combat. I pre-agree to the ceiling: CSS/tween + pooled sprite particles only, no shaders (constraint #2); the recap and currency-pill motion run **pre-run only** so they never contend with the live combat frame; currency pills are static DOM.
- **Not viewport/perf-feasible — flag from the survey benchmarks:**
  - **"Make the menu a *place*" (Takeaway 6 / `holocure-farm-hub.jpg`) cannot mean a walkable hub.** A navigable 3D space on static-Pages Three.js shared with combat is not feasible and would fight the learned dock (constraint #5). It stays a **framed diorama** (our existing live canvas + reactive FRONT/BACK companions), not a place you move through.
  - **VS stage-select's "everything visible at once" (`vampire-survivors-stage-selection.jpg`: stage list + full modifier panel + music panel + toggles simultaneously) is a ~1280px wide-screen read.** On a 273px mobile panel those regions cannot coexist without scroll-hell — mobile must **sequence** them (stage rail → selected briefing below, progressive-disclosed). The simultaneous loadout theater is a wide-tier luxury.
  - **The Soulstone radial skill-tree "spectacle" (Takeaway 4)** is a 성장-dock/desktop concern, out of scope here — but flag now that on mobile it can only be a vertical node ladder, never a true radial map; don't let the lobby direction imply a spectacle bar the phone can't meet.
- **Risk in my own proposal:** the currency rail + theatricalized toast are **new screen-space chrome**, and constraint #5 forbids relocating learned structure. Mitigation baked in: the rail is additive top-corner chrome that **deep-links into** the existing docks rather than duplicating any control, and the dock nav/tab structure (rail, tabs, FAB, close) is untouched.
